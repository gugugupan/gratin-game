# Brand & voice

**Account**: @gratingame — display name 「グラタンゲーム | Gratin Game」, Business account, Tokyo, link `https://gratin-game.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=profile`.
**Tagline**: 焼きたての HTML ゲーム、そろってます。 / Free bite-sized browser games, freshly baked.
**Logo**: the 「ー」 in ゲーム is a stretching cheese strand. Colors: crust #4A2814, terracotta #C9472D, cheese #F5B935, cream #FFF1D6. Font for generated cards: Dela Gothic One (`brand/src/`, limited glyphs — no ▶).

## Voice
- Japanese, friendly です/ます, short lines, one emoji per idea (🧀 brand, 🍋 Lemon Punch, 🧱 Clickton, 🔲 Gridlands, 🚂 trains, 🌙 night).
- Talk like the person making the games ("つくっています", "感想ぜんぶ読んでます"), not like an ad.
- End with a light invitation or question ("教えてください", "見せてください", "どれですか？").
- English posts only for visual content aimed at #indiedev / #screenshotsaturday; never mix three languages in one post.

Typical set: work tag + one player tag + `#ブラウザゲーム` (or `#個人開発` for dev-story posts).

## Never
- 「CCレモン」 anywhere in a post or tag (trademark).
- Numbers you didn't verify ("10分で町ができる" was flagged as an overclaim).
- Links in the main post of a promo — put them in the self-reply.
- Replies/likes/follows on other accounts on the user's behalf.
